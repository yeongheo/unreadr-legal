Unreadr Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Unreadr ("the app") is published by Yeong Heo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Information we collect

Account information. You sign in with Sign in with Apple. We receive an account identifier from Apple and the email address you choose to share (which may be Apple's private relay address). We do not receive your Apple ID password.

Your library. The books you save: title, author, cover image link, publisher, ISBN, description, publication date, your notes and tags, and the date you added each book.

Content you share with the app. When you share a post, link, text, or photos to Unreadr, or take or choose a photo in the app, that content is sent to our servers so the books in it can be identified. We keep a record of each import and its results.

Purchases. If you subscribe to Unreadr Pro, Apple processes the payment. We receive your subscription status and purchase history for the app, but never your card or payment details.

Usage counts. We count how many automatic imports your account has used, to apply the limits of the free and Pro plans.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

How we use information

  • To provide the app: saving your library and syncing it across your devices.
  • To identify books in the content you share and to find their details and covers.
  • To provide and manage subscriptions and apply plan limits.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep information

We keep your account, library, and import records until you delete them or delete your account.

Your choices

  • Delete a book: remove it in the app and it is deleted from our servers.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your library, and your import and usage records from our servers. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your App Store settings.
  • Access or correction: contact us at the address below.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your information, such as the right to request a copy of it or to object to how it is used. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access to your library is restricted to your account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Unreadr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
